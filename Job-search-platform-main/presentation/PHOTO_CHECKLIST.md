## 🎨 Photo Quality Tips

### **Technical Requirements:**
- **Resolution**: At least 1920x1080 for main screenshots
- **Format**: PNG for screenshots, JPG for photos
- **File Size**: Keep under 5MB each
- **Clarity**: Ensure text is readable when projected

### **Visual Guidelines:**
- **Clean Interface**: Close unnecessary browser tabs
- **Consistent Browser**: Use same browser for all web screenshots
- **Good Contrast**: Ensure screenshots are clear and readable
- **Professional Appearance**: Tidy up desktop/workspace before capturing

### **Screenshot Tools:**
1. **Windows Snipping Tool** (Built-in)
2. **Browser Screenshot** (Right-click → "Capture screenshot")
3. **Lightshot** (Free download)
4. **Greenshot** (Free, professional)
