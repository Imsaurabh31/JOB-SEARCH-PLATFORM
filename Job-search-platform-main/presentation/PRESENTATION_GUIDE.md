## 📋 What You Need to Do

### **Step 1: Take Screenshots (30 minutes)**
1. **Start your application**: `npm start`
2. **Follow PHOTO_CHECKLIST.md** - Take all 17 required screenshots
3. **Save images** in `presentation/images/` folder
4. **Use exact filenames** from the checklist

### **Step 2: Create PowerPoint Presentation (45 minutes)**
1. **Open PowerPoint** or Google Slides
2. **Use SLIDE_CONTENT.md** - Copy content for each of 21 slides
3. **Insert screenshots** from your images folder
4. **Apply consistent theme** (blue colors matching your app)
5. **Save as** `Job_Finder_Presentation.pptx` in `slides/` folder

### **Step 3: Prepare for Presentation (15 minutes)**
1. **Practice your demo** - Run through the app 2-3 times
2. **Test your slides** - Make sure all images display correctly
3. **Prepare backup** - Have screenshots ready if live demo fails
4. **Time yourself** - Aim for 10-15 minutes total
